versekit

Publish your own videos everywhere, from one place.

An open-source tool that runs on your own computer.

versekit keeps a library of the videos and posts you make, publishes them to the platforms you connect (TikTok, YouTube and others), and collects their performance so you can see what worked.

It is free software. You install it on your own computer and connect only your own accounts. Nobody else runs it for you, and your account credentials stay on your machine.
