Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

versekit is open-source software that runs on your own computer, or on a server you control. This policy explains what data versekit handles and where it goes.

1. The short version

The versekit project does not run a server that receives your data. Everything versekit stores is stored on machines you control. Data leaves those machines only to go to the platforms you connect, and to any optional services you configure yourself.

2. Data versekit handles

  • Your content: videos, images, titles, captions and other files you add to your library.
  • Platform authorization: when you connect an account (for example through TikTok Login Kit or Google OAuth), versekit receives access and refresh tokens from that platform.
  • Basic account information: with the TikTok user.info.basic permission, versekit reads your account's display name and avatar, and shows them so you can confirm which account you are posting to.
  • Publishing results and performance data: post IDs, publish status, and metrics such as views and likes that the platform returns for your own posts.

3. How it is used

versekit uses this data only to do what you ask: show your library, publish your content to the accounts you connected, and show you how your posts perform. It does not sell, rent or share your data, does not show ads, and does not include analytics or tracking that reports to us.

4. Where it is stored

Your library and history are stored in a local database on your computer, or on your own server if you set one up. Platform tokens are stored only on the computer that publishes for that account and are not sent anywhere except back to the platform that issued them.

5. Who receives your data

  • The platforms you connect: when you publish, your content and post settings are sent to that platform (for example TikTok or YouTube), under that platform's own privacy policy, such as the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Services you configure: if you choose to connect optional services, such as a chat app for notifications or an AI model provider for writing suggestions, versekit sends them only the data needed for that feature, using credentials you provide.

The versekit project and its contributors receive none of it.

6. Your control

  • Disconnect an account in versekit at any time; its stored tokens are deleted.
  • You can also revoke access from the platform itself, for example in TikTok's settings under Security and permissions, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting versekit's data directory removes everything it stored.

7. Children

versekit is not directed at children under 13, or the minimum age required by the platform you connect.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are published on this page with a new effective date, and the history is kept in the public repository.
