Set up this computer as your Hifabu publishing computer.

A publishing computer is the computer where Hifabu is installed and that publishes your confirmed videos on schedule to the platform accounts you connect. No account is needed to download.

把这台电脑设成 Hifabu 发布机。发布机是装了 Hifabu、负责到点发布你确认过的作品的那台电脑。下载不需要登录。

Before you install · 安装前先看

Put it on a computer on the same network as your phone

Install Hifabu on a computer that stays on the same network as the phone you use for publishing (for example, the same home broadband). Platforms treat signing in or posting from a different location or network as an account risk.

放在哪台电脑上（先于安装）：发布机要和你发布时用的手机在同一个网络（比如同一个家里的宽带）。平台会把「手机和电脑不在一个地方」当成风险。

System requirements

  • macOS 13.5 or later (Apple silicon and Intel both supported).
  • About 300 MB of free disk space for Hifabu and its runtime, plus space for your videos.
  • Always on and online when scheduled posts are due (closing a laptop lid pauses scheduled publishing).
  • Linux (arm64 and x64) works and is tested, though macOS is the primary target. Windows is not supported.

系统要求：macOS 13.5 或更新（Apple 芯片和 Intel 都支持）；约 300 MB 空间，另加存放视频的空间；需要保持开机联网（合上笔记本盖子会暂停排期发布）。Linux 可用，但不作为主推平台；不支持 Windows。

What gets installed

Core installation only downloads a private Node.js runtime and the Hifabu app. Platform components are downloaded on demand only when you first connect that service. Every file is checked against its locked SHA-256 before unpacking.

What runs in the background

Hifabu registers 1 user background service, com.hifabu.daemon. It starts when you sign in to macOS and restarts automatically if it crashes. Specifically, it:

  • Serves the local web console on 127.0.0.1 only (not reachable from other devices).
  • Checks Hifabu every 5 minutes for new finished videos sent from your editing computer.
  • Publishes only the posts you have confirmed when their scheduled time arrives.
  • Checks every 6 hours for a new official release and updates while idle (you can turn auto-update off on the System page).

Service logs are written inside ~/.local/state/versekit/ (legacy folder name, will be renamed later). Beyond this single service, Hifabu does not change system settings, does not install browser extensions, and never asks for your administrator password.

后台只常驻 1 个用户服务 com.hifabu.daemon，开机自动启动、崩溃自动拉起，负责：在本机 127.0.0.1 提供控制台；每 5 分钟取一次新作品；到点发布你确认过的作品；每 6 小时检查一次正式版本并在空闲时自动更新（可在系统页关掉）。日志放在 ~/.local/state/versekit/（旧名，之后会改）。除此之外，不改系统设置，不装浏览器插件，不要管理员密码。

How to uninstall

Open the publishing computer console and go to System → Uninstall Hifabu. By default, Hifabu removes the background service and app files while keeping your data (platform logins, videos, and schedule). You can also choose to remove data together.

If you want to remove local files manually, Hifabu stores files in these folders (legacy folder names, will be renamed later):

  • Program & private runtime: ~/.versekit
  • Config & credentials: ~/.config/versekit
  • Database, browser logins & logs: ~/.local/state/versekit

After uninstalling, open My Hifabu and remove this computer from your connected devices.

在发布机控制台打开「系统 → 卸载 Hifabu」，默认保留数据（账号登录、作品、排期），也可以选「连数据一起删」。手动清理可删除上面三个目录（旧名，之后会改）。卸载后别忘了到「我的 Hifabu」把这台机器移除。
